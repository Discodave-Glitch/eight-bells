# Eight Bells 3.1.0

**The Stage**: a full-screen animation overlay with 320 scenes that move to your live tracks and patterns. Nothing here changes what is sent to the FM-1; the MIDI is byte-for-byte the same.

## New
- 320 scenes, from cars, animals, food and funfairs to everyday objects such as umbrellas, compasses, balloons and garden tools. Each reacts to the eight tracks (kick, snare, hat, perc, bass, chord, lead, sample).
- Holds still when nothing is playing and winds down smoothly on Stop.
- Full screen fits without cropping or black edges, with Play/Stop and Previous/Next buttons. It starts by itself when a phone or tablet is turned sideways.

## Fixes
- Marble maze can now be completed; bobsleigh no longer takes off; Santa's sleigh and reindeer the right way round; LCD scene shows the BPM.

## Downloads
- Windows: `Eight-Bells-3.1.0-portable.exe` (no install) or `Eight-Bells-Setup-3.1.0.exe`.
- Mac: `Eight-Bells-3.1.0-x64.dmg` (Intel) or `Eight-Bells-3.1.0-arm64.dmg` (Apple silicon).
- The apps are not code-signed: on Windows choose More info, then Run anyway; on Mac right-click and choose Open the first time.
