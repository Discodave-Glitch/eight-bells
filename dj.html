Eight Bells DJprototype

Two decks, each making its own tune, with jog platters, a 3-band EQ, filters, a crossfader and punch pads. It plays through this device's speakers or headphones. It does not use the FM-1. Works with a MIDI DJ controller such as the Numark Party Mix Live (use Chrome or Edge on a computer or Android).

Guided setup walks through the controls one by one. When a control lights up yellow, press or move the matching control on your deck. Tap Skip for any it does not have. Your choices are remembered on this device. Jog wheel: turn it to nudge the tempo. CUE: hold it to preview, or tap it while playing to restart from the top. Pads button cycles Effects 1-4, 5-8 and Patterns 1-4, 5-8. Double-tap an EQ or filter knob to centre it.

The SMC-PAD still works as before: pads 36 to 67, knobs 1 to 8 on CC 30 to 37, CC 25 to 29 for play and stop.
