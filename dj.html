Eight Bells DJprototype, separate from the main app

AB

Double-tap an EQ or filter slider to put it back to the middle. Bottom two rows are Deck A, top two rows are Deck B. Hold an effects pad to keep the effect on.

Knobs 1 to 8 (CC 30 to 37): Deck A volume, Deck B volume, Deck A filter, Deck B filter, crossfader, Deck A tempo, Deck B tempo, master volume. Buttons: CC 25 Deck A play, CC 26 Deck B play, CC 27 play both, CC 28 stop both, CC 29 sync.
