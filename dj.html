Eight Bells DJprototype

Two decks, each making its own tune, with jog platters, a 3-band EQ, filters, a crossfader and punch pads. It plays through this device's speakers or headphones. It does not use the FM-1. Made for the SMC-PAD, and any other MIDI controller can be taught with MIDI learn (use Chrome or Edge on a computer or Android).

Guided setup walks through the controls one by one. When a control lights up yellow, press or move the matching control on your deck. Tap Skip for any it does not have. Your choices are remembered on this device. Jog wheel: turn it to nudge the tempo. CUE: hold it to preview, or tap it while playing to restart from the top. Double-tap an EQ or filter knob to centre it.

SMC-PAD pads: Bank A pads are effects, Bank B pads are patterns (the pad's own Bank button picks). Pads 1 to 8 (bottom two rows) are Deck A and pads 9 to 16 (top two rows) are Deck B, read left to right: Intro, Build-up, Drop, Chorus, then Break, Bridge, Half, Outro (effects: Loop 1/4, Loop 1/8, Stutter, Reverse, then Tape stop, Low-pass, High-pass, Echo). Knob bank A (CC 30 to 37): knob 1 crossfader, knob 2 master volume, then the EQ with Deck A on the odd knobs and Deck B on the even knobs: Bass 3 and 4, Mid 5 and 6, Treble 7 and 8. Knob bank B (CC 38 to 45, or the numbers you taught Eight Bells Pad mode on this device): knobs 1 and 2 the same crossfader and master volume, then volumes 3 and 4, filters 5 and 6, tempos 7 and 8. Buttons: < and > (CC 25, 26) sync Deck A to Deck B and Deck B to Deck A, Play (27) and Stop (28) run both decks, Record (29) syncs Deck B to Deck A. If bank B does nothing on your pad, use Learn one control to teach it.
